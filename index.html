Scenario Alignment

Notes on the Worst-Case Scenario Model

The worst-case document is a useful stress frame, and showing negative cells builds confidence. Two refinements would make it a shared planning basis.
ItemSuggested refinement
Price / volume correlationLow-volume scenarios should pair with conservative pricing rather than the $82.50 premium average — slow placements rarely command top-of-range prices.
Spoilage at low volumeShrink rises as turns slow; adding a tiered spoilage line (we model 15% at 1.5/day) would make the floor scenarios more robust.
Restocking laborThe cleaning-route synergy is real; we are running a time study to establish the true marginal cost and will share the result.

Where this leaves us

On shared conservative assumptions the business clears financing at every volume tier. Supply chain structure and placement selection are the two items that turn a good model into a great program — both are on the table for our next conversation.
All projections are estimates based on stated assumptions. Actual results will vary by location, foot traffic, product mix, and operational efficiency. This document is not a guarantee of performance.
