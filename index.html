Where the review stands

Open Items

Every note attaches to the line it is about. Open items are questions still waiting on an answer; answered means someone replied; resolved means both sides are done with it. Click any item to land on that row with the thread open.

How the review layer works

Enter the review passphrase once per session. On either document, + Add note then click the row, zone or section you are commenting on — that row highlights as you hover, and the note stays attached to it whatever screen it is read on. Click anywhere else for a general note.

Threads open in a side panel that never covers the document. Anyone can reply, mark a thread answered or resolved, and export the full log grouped by line item. The documents themselves are readable without the passphrase — it only controls the review layer.

All projections are estimates based on stated assumptions. Actual results will vary by location, foot traffic, product mix, and operational efficiency. Nothing here is a guarantee of performance.
