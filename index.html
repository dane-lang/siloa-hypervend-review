Shared notes on both documents

The pro forma and the placement matrix each carry a shared sticky-note layer. Enter the review passphrase once and the toolbar appears at the bottom right: pick your name, place a note anywhere on the page, drag it where it belongs, and it shows up for anyone else reading the same page within a couple of seconds. Notes stay with the page they were left on, and the whole set can be exported to a text file.

The documents themselves are readable without the passphrase — it only controls the note layer.

All projections are estimates based on stated assumptions. Actual results will vary by location, foot traffic, product mix, and operational efficiency. Nothing here is a guarantee of performance.
